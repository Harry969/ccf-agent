\section{Algorithm}

\subsection{Problem Framing}
State the task, input, output, constraints, and objective. The reader should know what object the algorithm is trying to build and what counts as success.

\subsection{Baseline And Bottleneck}
Describe the direct solution and explain exactly why it is too slow, too brittle, or too incomplete.

\subsection{Core Observation}
State the invariant, monotonicity, exchange argument, recurrence, or structural property that makes the method possible.

\subsection{Algorithm Design}
Define the maintained state, the update rule, the procedure, and the data structures. Introduce notation before it is used in later paragraphs or pseudocode.

\subsection{Correctness}
Prove that the procedure returns the required result. Match the proof to the object actually built in the algorithm, not to a hypothetical cleaner version.

\subsection{Complexity}
Define every variable that appears in the bound and give time and space complexity in terms of those variables.

\subsection{Implementation Details And Edge Cases}
List boundary conditions, empty inputs, tie cases, and common implementation mistakes.
