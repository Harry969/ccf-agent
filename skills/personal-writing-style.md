# Personal Writing Style Skill

This skill keeps the draft close to a calm, technical, author-like voice.

## Voice

- Clear, restrained, and explanatory.
- Prefer "we first observe..." only when the observation is actually stated.
- Prefer concrete transitions over slogans.
- Let the proof and examples carry confidence.

## Rhythm

- Start sections by locating the reader's difficulty.
- Use short definitions before longer derivations.
- Alternate explanation, formula, and consequence so the reader can keep orientation.
- End paragraphs with a useful technical consequence, not a decorative sentence.

## Avoid

- Marketing phrases such as "powerful", "revolutionary", or "state of the art" without evidence.
- Empty certainty such as "obviously", "clearly", or "it is easy to see" when a proof step is needed.
- Overly casual contest-solution slang unless the target context explicitly asks for it.
- Fabricated citations, numbers, ablations, or benchmark results.
