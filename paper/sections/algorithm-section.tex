\section{Algorithm Section Template}

\subsection{Problem Framing}
State the problem, input, output, and constraints.

\subsection{Baseline And Bottleneck}
Explain the direct solution and why its time or space complexity is insufficient.

\subsection{Core Observation}
State the observation that makes the algorithm possible.

\subsection{Algorithm Design}
Define the state, transition, data structure, or procedure.

\subsection{Correctness}
Prove that the method returns the required answer.

\subsection{Complexity}
Analyze time and space complexity with named variables.

\subsection{Implementation Details And Edge Cases}
Record boundary conditions and implementation pitfalls.
